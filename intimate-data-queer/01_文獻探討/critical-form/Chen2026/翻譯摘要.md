## 原文 Abstract

Taiwanese women negotiate menstrual tracking apps at the intersection of algorithmic systems, cultural expectations, and embodied experiences. This study harnessed qualitative digital methods combining diary studies and in-depth interviews with sixteen participants to develop intimate data performativity as a conceptual lens. Three interconnected dimensions emerged. First, algorithmic negotiation in embodied knowledge production: users embrace predictive systems while maintaining interpretive agency. Second, integration of tracking into self-optimization practices exposes contradictory dynamics of empowerment and control. Third, data sharing reconfigures intimate relationships as menstrual apps mediate gendered dynamics of care and surveillance. Women actively co-constitute bodily knowledge, regulatory norms, and relationship dynamics through everyday engagement with menstrual tracking technologies. These performative practices exceed binary frameworks of empowerment or discipline.

## 中文翻譯

台灣女性在演算法系統、文化期待與具身經驗交會之處，協商經期追蹤 App 的使用。本研究採用結合日記研究法與深度訪談的質化數位方法，訪談 16 位參與者，發展出「親密資料展演性」（intimate data performativity）作為概念透鏡。研究浮現三個相互關聯的維度。第一，具身知識生產中的演算法協商：使用者一方面擁抱預測系統，一方面仍保有詮釋自主權。第二，追蹤整合進自我優化實踐，暴露出賦權與控制並存的矛盾動態。第三，資料分享重構親密關係，經期 App 中介了照顧與監控的性別化動態。女性透過日常使用經期追蹤科技，主動共同建構身體知識、規範常規與關係動態。這些展演性實踐超越了賦權或規訓的二元框架。
